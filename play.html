Psycle

I (and AI) launched my iOS app! I was missing a few features from current period tracker apps: adding my own cycle-synced self-help tricks and setting reminders for myself and my partner. So I delved into my favourite topic: Femtech. I researched, interviewed women, I gathered incredible insights. I went to workshops to validate the content and developed my iOS app. My partner is a fan, it has become a relationship booster. ❤︎

Tools I used: Claude code, Cursor, A0.dev, Figma, Midjourney.

Root Stress Simulator

Maple Roots is an interactive web experience that puts you underground with a Berlin street tree.

You control three urban stressors, urine loading, heat, and rain, and watch how they reshape the root zone in a tiny concrete tree pit. As the conditions shift, the site responds with visuals, ambient sound, and short first-person stories from the roots themselves: what it feels like to live beneath a Kreuzberg sidewalk when parties, heatwaves, and weather collide in the same confined soil.
