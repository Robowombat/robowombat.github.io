Hello, I’m Petra.

I am a full-stack designer,BerlinremoteanywhereBerlin

I design products and craft visuals with attitude since 2008. Otherwise, you’ll find me praising Japanese food, vibe-coding, or obsessing over femtech.

I (and AI) launched my native app! It's been my love project: I had a problem to solve: I was missing a few features from current  period tracker apps.:  adding my own cycle-synced self-help tricks and setting reminders for myself and my partner. So I dwelled into my favourite topic: Femtech. I researched, interviewed women, I  gathered incredible insights. I went to workshops to validate the content and developed my iOS app.

Tools I used: Claude code, Cursor, A0.dev, Figma, Midjourney.
Status: Ready in the Appstore!
