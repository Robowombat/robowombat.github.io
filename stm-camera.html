ShareTheMeal brand, icons and animations: UI case study

"ShareTheMeal has bright, charming graphics, an effortless interface..."

- Michael Kors / Source‍

ShareTheMeal

UX/UI

company

ShareTheMeal by the United Nations World Food Programme.

My role

UI/UX designer: Leading the Product brand appearance, Drawing, animating, Crafting User Interface assets, iconography, illustrations and animation, building a design system.

OUTCOME

I created not just an app, but a holistic, engaging experience that began before users opened it and continued long after they closed it. The ShareTheMeal brand became instantly recognizable, with its distinctive assets and tone consistently reflected across all platforms.

overview

ShareTheMeal is a crowdfunding mobile app by the UN World Food Programme that fights global hunger. It enables users to make small or large donations to WFP emergency missions and track their progress worldwide.

AWARDS

Google Play Award in 2017, Best Apps of 2020 by Google and Apple. SXSW Interactive Innovation Award, GLOMO Award 2017 - Outstanding Mobile Contribution, The Webby People's Voice Award, was a finalist @Classy Award and @Cannes Lions.

IMPACT

Scaled the product between 2016–2019 from 600,000 to over 1 million users, and from 4 to 13 languages. Increased the number of shared meals from 8 million to over 35 million. The app raised global awareness and was featured in prominent outlets such as Times Square, BBC, and Al Jazeera.
