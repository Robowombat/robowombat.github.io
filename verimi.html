Designing Trust: Simplifying 2FA for Digital Identity with Verimi

Overview

Verimi securely stores your personal data and ID documents in a digital ID Wallet, ready to use anytime. Identify yourself online, log in, sign, or pay with just one click. Verimi enables access to digital services across industries, all while putting security and user control at the forefront.

My role

Jul 2019 - Feb 2021
UX/UI Designer, responsible for developing cross-platform user flows for: Login with 2-Factor Authentication, Account Activation, Account Recovery.

Client

Verimi ID Wallet
‍Integration Partners: Deutsche Bank, Deutsche Telekom, Volkswagen Financial Services

The challenge

Making third-party integration frictionless

One core question kept surfacing:
How prominent should Verimi’s brand be during a partner-branded login flow?
‍Take the Deutsche Bank use case:
Would showcasing Verimi too much interfere with the user's primary goal of logging in quickly and securely? Did users need to consciously engage with Verimi as a brand during that process?

These were the questions I continuously explored.
‍
The technological foundation was already defined: a standalone, secure 2FA flow.
My first task was to understand the product’s value, visibility, and discoverability, and how it fits into different brand ecosystems.
Through qualitative user testing, two main issues emerged:
• Users were unaware of the full scope and benefits of the Verimi Wallet (which enables login, identification, payment, and legal digital signatures).
• The initial integration flow (e.g., logging in with 2FA) felt overly technical, long, and confusing, especially for first-time users.

The product promised secure access for everyone.
So, the challenge was to simplify the experience, to keep the product  complex but not complicated.
, reducing clicks, removing technical friction, and making a complex product feel intuitive.

The Solution

Shortened, smoother 2FA flow

I redesigned the core user journey with a focus on clarity and simplicity:
• Clean UI layouts with clear next steps
• Supportive micro-animations for account activation and recovery
• Shortened steps with fewer interruptions
• Informative visuals to support comprehension without heavy text
• Animations were especially helpful in creating smooth transitions, clarifying steps, and helping users feel more in control.

The solution

Discoverability

A key insights:
many users simply didn’t understand what Verimi could offer, until it was too late. Once the value clicked, feedback was overwhelmingly positive (source: user interviews).
The next step was to tell Verimi’s story in a way that felt immediate and relevant to everyday life. I developed a light, fast, and user-friendly animation concept that could explain the wallet’s core benefits and features.
This storytelling format could be used across:
• Product onboarding,
• The Verimi website
• Points of integration (POIs) with partners

By translating abstract benefits into relatable scenarios, we elevated both understanding and engagement, without adding friction.
