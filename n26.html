Transparent Mastercard. Transparent banking.

Overview

A 100% digital banking experience that’s designed to be simple and transparent. It’s an app that feels good to use, integrating the most innovative technology to make life easier, and enabling you to live and bank your way.

My role:

Brand & UI designer. I designed & delivered their first transparent Mastercard & Print Carriers & Branded Goodies.

Client:

N26 The Mobile Bank, Berlin

The Tools

As part of my design process I conducted a Design Sprint to explore and ideate together with a team of 11 participants.

  • User journey Mapping based on 2 user types
  • Defining Sprint questions
  • 4 step sketches (Notes, Sketches, Crazy 8, Solution design, Presenting)
  • Low-fi prototyping
  • Building interactive prototypes for testing

The challenge

The transparent Mastercard

The essential part of the brand is the transparent mastercard. After visiting the Mastercard producer factory in Paderborn, Germany, and dealing with numerous technical challenges and adjustments we finally produced the first fancy Number26 transparent bank cards.

Also, I designed a premium Carrier for the home delivered bank cards ensuring a high-end experience for the earliest Number26 users.​The original logo of Number26 represented a versatile shape with 26 visible sides of cubes. ​
