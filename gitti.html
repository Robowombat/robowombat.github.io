Client

Gitti - A Conscious Beauty Brand from Berlin that rethinks industry standards with vegan, PETA-certified products rooted in nature and science.

My role

Freelance UX/UI designer:
• Redesigning Gitti's Product page for single and collection products.
• Redesigning Gitti's Cart drawer.

The problem

Optimize for the user & for the business

The previous PDP had many problems with its informaton architecture, relavance of content (data based on hotjar user behaviour scans and direct customer feedback).

Key Pain Points Identified on the PDP
‍
• The current set configuration layout is not scalable, making it difficult to accommodate more complex bundles or product combinations.

‍Color Selection & Product Variants
• Color picker is placed too low on the page, causing unnecessary scrolling and disrupting the user flow.It’s unclear that the color options are swipable, reducing discoverability and interactivity.

‍Gallery & Visual Presentation
• Product image gallery doesn’t support full-screen view, limiting the immersive experience.
• The number of image dots is excessive, overwhelming users without offering navigation support.
• Some galleries show cropped images, requiring extra swipes to view a full product, which affects usability.

‍Pricing & Value Communication
• Product price is displayed in a small font size, making it hard to notice or scan quickly.
• Free delivery information is not prominently displayed, even when the cart value qualifies, leading to missed conversion incentives.
‍
‍Set Customization & Flow Interruptions
• Users are redirected to a new page when trying to customize sets, causing flow interruptions and potential drop-offs.

‍Information Architecture & Terminology
• Key elements like “Geschenk” (gift) are not explained well or lead to confusing links, reducing clarity.
• Sections like product details and application are text-heavy and could benefit from more visual or collapsible formatting.
‍
Delivery Expectations & UX Enhancements
• Expected delivery times are not clearly displayed, missing an important trust-building detail.There’s no clear indication of how many users can add favourites, or how logged-in states affect the experience.

The solution

A Clear UX, scalable to all products

The redesigned PDP focuses on a smoother, more intuitive shopping experience by streamlining product customization, improving visual hierarchy, and making key actions like color selection and add-to-cart more accessible. Enhanced layout scalability, clearer delivery information, and a more immersive gallery help build trust and support conversion across devices.

Optimizations Made in the Redesigned PDP:

‍Improved Product Configuration Flow
‍Users can now easily customize product sets or shades directly on the PDP without being redirected, streamlining the experience.

‍More Prominent Visual Hierarchy
‍Key elements such as price, free delivery, and product benefits are now visually emphasized and easier to scan.

‍Enhanced Gallery Usability
‍The product image gallery is more intuitive with clearer navigation and larger, uncropped visuals, improving product perception.

‍Sticky Add-to-Cart Section
‍The redesigned version includes a persistent add-to-cart area to reduce friction and encourage quicker conversions.

‍Clearer Color Picker & Swatches
‍Swatches are now more visible and clearly interactive (swipable or clickable), enhancing product variant selection.

‍Modular, Scalable Layout
‍The page structure is more modular, making it scalable for different product types and compatible with future bundles or upsells.

‍Stronger Trust & Transparency Elements
‍Delivery times, ingredient highlights, and certifications (e.g., vegan, PETA-certified) are now more visible and accessible.

‍Cleaner Mobile Experience
‍The mobile layout is less fragmented, with smoother transitions between sections and reduced vertical overload.

Redesigning Cart Drawer

In the new basket design, I optimized the user experience by introducing a persistent sidebar cart that allows seamless browsing while viewing the basket. I added clear product grouping for sets, visible discounts, and a progress bar to encourage upselling. The design now includes upsell suggestions, a free gift selection, and a gift bag add-on to increase order value. I also integrated a promo code field with a pre-filled suggestion, added an estimated delivery date, and displayed trusted payment options to boost conversion and checkout confidence.
